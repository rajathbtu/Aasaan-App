How it works

Three steps, under a minute

From request to a provider on the phone. Built for speed on patchy networks and entry-level phones.

  1. 01

    Describe the job

    Pick from 21 services, add a couple of tags that describe the actual problem — "switch board", "MCB tripping" — and confirm your location. Auto-detected, but editable if it's wrong.

    Service + tags + location
  2. 02

    Nearby providers see it

    Your request lands in the feed of providers who do that work, whose radius covers your area. Sorted by distance, so the closest and most relevant people see it first.

    Matched by service and distance
  3. 03

    Talk, hire, rate

    Accept one and call them directly — no commission, no middleman, no awkward negotiation through an app. When the job is done, close the request and rate the provider.

    Direct call, then ratings

Everything inside

Everything the app does today

No roadmap, no waitlist. This is what ships in Aasaan right now.

4-digit OTP login

Sign up with a mobile number and a 4-digit code. No passwords, no email, no waiting. Truecaller one-tap login is supported wherever it's available.

Location & radius matching

Your location is detected automatically and stays editable. Providers only ever see work inside the radius they set themselves.

Live tracking

See the request and the provider on a map, so both sides know what's happening.

Request boosting

Pay to raise a request's visibility when the job genuinely can't wait.

Razorpay payments

Secure checkout in-app and on the web, with every order and payment tracked.

Push notifications

Instant alerts for new requests, acceptances and every status change.

Ratings & reviews

Rate after the work is done. Providers can chase a rating once 4 hours have passed.

AI voice calling

Sarvam-powered agents handle outbound and realtime calls in your language.

WhatsApp updates

Meta Cloud API with inbound webhooks, so updates reach you where you already are.

Offline-tolerant

Cached services, offline indicators, and graceful empty and error states.

Low data, low-end ready

A fast, light UI that works on the phone you already own. Android, iOS and web ship from one codebase.
