## 1. Information We Collect and Process

The information we process depends on how you use Aasaanly and which features you choose.

### 1.1 Account and Sign-In Information

We process your:

- Mobile number.
- Full name.
- Aasaanly account identifier.
- Account role (service requester or service provider).
- Selected language.
- Authentication and account-related information.

We use one-time passwords (OTPs) for phone verification.

If you choose Truecaller sign-in, Truecaller may provide authentication and profile information, including a verified phone number, through its sign-in flow. Information processed by Truecaller is also subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

### 1.2 Service Provider Profile Information

If you use Aasaanly as a service provider, we may process:

- Selected services or professional categories.
- Service locations and service radius.
- Profile photo and biography.
- Work experience year.
- Birth year and gender, where provided.
- Subscription or professional plan details.
- Credit points balance.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Other information voluntarily provided as part of your profile.

Profile photos may be reviewed by Aasaanly moderators before being displayed to other users.

Certain profile information, including your name, photo, services offered, experience and ratings, may be visible to relevant customers to help them evaluate service providers.

### 1.3 Location and Work Requests

When you create a work request, we process information such as:

- Service requested.
- Optional request title and description.
- Selected tags.
- Location name and coordinates.
- Request status and creation date.
- Phone-sharing preference associated with the request.

The app may use GPS coordinates if you grant foreground location permission.

If GPS access is unavailable, the app may request an approximate location based on your IP address through services such as ipapi.co or ipinfo.io.

Place searches and location suggestions may be processed through Google Places or Google Maps.

Location information is used to identify and match service providers within a relevant area and to display or manage work requests.

Eligible service providers may see relevant request information, including the service category, description, tags and approximate location, as necessary to evaluate and respond to the request.

We do not intend to disclose a requester's precise GPS coordinates to service providers.

Your phone number is shared with a service provider only when:

1. You provide specific phone-sharing consent for that work request; and
2. The service provider has successfully completed Aasaanly's mandatory Aadhaar-based identity verification requirement for customer calls.

You should avoid including personal or sensitive information in work requests that is not necessary to arrange the service.

### 1.4 Aadhaar eKYC Information

Aasaanly uses Aadhaar-based electronic Know Your Customer (eKYC) verification for service providers as part of its identity-verification process.

If you choose to complete Aadhaar eKYC, the Aadhaar number and OTP you submit are transmitted through the configured eKYC provider to initiate and complete the verification process.

Aasaanly's current application database is designed not to store the full Aadhaar number. It stores:

- The last four digits of the Aadhaar number.
- Verification status.
- An encrypted eKYC identity payload that may contain information such as your name, date of birth, gender and address, subject to the information returned by the verification provider.

Aadhaar verification is used to establish identity verification status and enable specified platform features, including customer calls to verified service providers.

The verification process does not constitute a police or criminal background check, professional qualification assessment, licence verification or guarantee of a service provider's competence, conduct, character or service quality.

Aadhaar verification information is processed subject to applicable Aadhaar laws and regulations, the relevant eKYC provider's terms and privacy practices, and the consent obtained from you.

Aadhaar OTPs and authentication input information are not intended to be retained in Aasaanly's application database or ordinary application logs.

### 1.5 Contacts and Contact Discovery

On supported Android devices, Aasaanly may request optional access to your device contacts to help identify potential service providers, such as electricians, plumbers, maids, carpenters and other professionals.

If you grant contacts permission:

1. The app examines contact names and relevant contact details locally on your device.
2. It identifies contacts matching predefined service-related keywords.
3. Relevant matching contact information, such as names and phone numbers, may be uploaded to Aasaanly's backend to support provider discovery and onboarding.

Aasaanly does not need to upload your entire address book for this matching process.

Contacts access is optional. You can deny or revoke this permission through your device settings.

Denying contacts access does not prevent you from using the core Services, although contact-based discovery features may not be available.

Contact information is used for provider discovery, potential onboarding and related platform operations. It is not intended to be used for unrelated purposes.

We take steps to limit the contact information collected to what is relevant to these purposes and handle it in accordance with applicable law.

### 1.6 Payments and Paid Features

When you purchase a request boost or service-provider plan, we process payment-related records such as:

- Order and payment identifiers.
- Amount and currency.
- Payment status.
- Purchase type.
- Associated account or work request.
- Transaction references and timestamps.

Razorpay or another payment provider identified at checkout processes the payment transaction and payment instrument information under its own privacy and security terms.

Aasaanly's application database is designed to store payment references and transaction details, not full card credentials or card security codes.

We also process credit points balances, earned points, redemption records and related transactions for eligible Aasaanly features.

Credit points do not expire, are non-transferable and cannot be converted into cash. They can be redeemed against eligible Aasaanly features at the applicable conversion rate displayed in the app.

### 1.7 Calls, Messages and Notifications

For supported onboarding and service communications, we may process your phone number and send SMS or WhatsApp messages.

Some communications may use a Sarvam AI voice agent.

When an AI voice-agent call is used:

- Call audio is streamed to Sarvam AI or its designated service infrastructure to facilitate the conversation.
- Aasaanly does not store audio recordings of these calls.
- Aasaanly does not store call transcripts.
- We may retain operational call metadata, such as phone numbers, call identifiers, call direction, status, timestamps and duration, where necessary for service operations, troubleshooting, security or dispute resolution.

The voice-agent service provider may process call audio temporarily as necessary to deliver the service, subject to its applicable terms, privacy practices and contractual arrangements.

Users may be informed when interacting with an AI voice assistant, including through relevant call announcements or notices.

Do not provide sensitive personal information during an AI-assisted call unless it is necessary for the stated service.

If you enable push notifications, we process your Expo push token, device platform and token update information to deliver notifications.

We also process in-app notification content and read status.

### 1.8 Device, App and Local-Storage Information

The app stores authentication and profile information on your device or browser and may cache information to support app operation.

On supported mobile devices, authentication data uses the platform secure-storage integration.

The website stores your language preference in browser local storage.

We also receive technical information necessary to make requests to our Services, protect accounts and diagnose service problems.

### 1.9 Analytics and App Usage Information

Aasaanly uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to understand how users interact with the application and to improve the Services.

Depending on the app configuration, analytics information may include:

- App screen views and navigation activity.
- Work request creation and related interactions.
- Work request acceptance and status changes.
- Profile updates and role switching.
- Subscription and purchase-related events.
- Notification interactions.
- Location and contacts permission events.
- OTP verification success or failure events.
- App version, device platform and technical information.
- App instance identifiers and other analytics-related identifiers.

Analytics events help us understand product usage, identify technical issues, measure feature adoption and improve the user experience.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect certain app usage and device information as part of its standard functionality, depending on the SDK configuration.

Google processes information received through Firebase Analytics under its applicable privacy terms.

We do not use Firebase Analytics as a means of selling personal data or authorizing unrelated third-party use of personal information.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain the necessary consent or offer applicable choices regarding analytics processing.
