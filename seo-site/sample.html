What to tell your electrician

A clear request can help a provider understand the work before responding. Include:

  • The affected item: fan, light, switchboard, socket or circuit.
  • The symptoms: not working, intermittent power or repeated MCB trips.
  • When it started: today or an ongoing issue.
  • The location: your area in Jhansi and precise service address in the request flow.
  • Useful context: property type, access requirements or a photo if the app supports safe photo sharing.
Electrical safety

If you notice smoke, sparks, a burning smell or exposed live wiring, stay away from the affected equipment. If safe to do so, keep others away and switch off the relevant supply without approaching the hazard. For fire or immediate danger, contact emergency services. Do not touch exposed wires or attempt unsafe repairs.
